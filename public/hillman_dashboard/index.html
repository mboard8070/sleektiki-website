Five-year turnaround trajectory

Revenue was flat-to-soft through the post-SPAC home-improvement cool-down; profitability and leverage did the heavy lifting. GAAP swung from a 2023 loss to $40M profit in 2025; Adj. EBITDA margin expanded ~280 bps from 2023 to 2025.

What the numbers say

  • Sales: $1.43B → $1.55B (2021–2025). Growth re-accelerated in 2025 after flat 2022–24.
  • Adj. EBITDA: $210M (2022) → $275M (2025), margin 14.9% → 17.7% (2023–25).
  • GAAP NI: $(9.6)M (2023) → $17.3M (2024) → $40.3M (2025).
  • Leverage: Net debt / Adj. EBITDA improved to 2.4× (from 2.8× YE24; company previously cited 4.2× YE22).
  • COGS (ex-D&A): 56.1% of sales (2023) → 51.3% (2025) — pricing/mix/tariff recovery timing.

Customer concentration stress tool

FY2025: Home Depot $349.9M (22.5%), Lowe’s $324.4M (20.9%), top-two $674.3M (~43%). Model a shared volume shock at big-box without assuming replacement wins.

Baseline sales $1,552.2M. Adj. EBITDA base $275.3M. ΔEBITDA ≈ (net sales impact) × contribution margin. Offset only applies to lost big-box volume. CM is a modeling assumption (not disclosed) — default 35% sits between gross margin ex-D&A (~48.7%) and Adj. EBITDA margin (17.7%).

China tariff sensitivity tool

10-K: ~33% China / 33% North America / 33% other product sourcing. Tariffs raise COGS and working capital; management says prices have been raised to offset, with possible demand impact.

Base COGS ex-D&A $795.9M (51.3% of sales). China COGS ≈ share × base COGS. Gross tariff cost = China COGS × tariff rate. Net cost after pass-through = cost × (1 − pass-through). Price uplift on total sales ≈ (gross tariff × pass-through) / base sales. Volume loss ≈ price uplift % × elasticity. Lost contribution uses CM from concentration tool.
